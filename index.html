The work

What we are building

Four products, each one aimed at a moment when a person is on their own and the stakes are real. Status is stated plainly on every one.

ProsaY

Court preparation
In rebuild

Have your SAY in court.

ProsaY walks a person through building a case in one clear flow, from the first fact to a finished filing. It was designed from the experience of walking into a courtroom unprepared, so nobody else has to.

  • Prepare. Practice. Perform. Build the case, rehearse in the courtroom simulator, then export and walk in ready.
  • Case Builder is step zero. Evidence Vault, document review, and a complete motion library live inside the flow.
  • Verification before trust. Claims get checked before they ever reach a filing.
  • Now in a full Flutter rebuild with the web demo live for demonstrations.

Spades Blast

Competitive spades game
In development

Cosmic Competitive Spades

A competitive spades game built for players who take the table seriously. Climb from NEWBIE to QUANTUM, play in first person at a living table, and feel every trick land.

  • Modes: ranked 2v2, 1v1, and free for all, plus tournaments, duel draft, and practice against AI.
  • Free to play with cosmetics. Ranked play is subscribers only at $4.99 a month.
  • Your rank stays yours. A lapsed subscription pauses ranked play and never wipes your rank.
  • Built to the master spec, milestone by milestone, with the trick zone always in clear sight.

X-plore

Dating and connection
In development

Powered by the Clair engine

A dating app built around sincerity instead of swiping volume. The Clair engine matches on resonance, and the design protects attention. Attention is sacred here, never flooded.

  • No likes anywhere. Ten sparks a day total, whether you spark or ghost, so every choice means something.
  • Touch rituals are rare by design. A weekly ritual goes only to someone you truly want to reach.
  • Whispers change with the sender. The conversation takes on the color of the person who wrote last.
  • Verified people only. Liveness checks at every level, with stronger verification as access grows.
